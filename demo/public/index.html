How the contract decides

  • Compliant when average pay for women is more than 95% of average pay for men.
  • Exactly 5% counts as non-compliant, matching "5% or more" in the directive.
  • Each group needs at least two people, so a verdict can't expose one person's pay.
  • Up to 8 people per audit.
